Cinematic website mockups

High-fidelity mockups of three concepts from CinematicWebsiteConcepts.md. Each one shows the hero and the two most important sections that follow it. Scroll, tap and ask.

Concept A

KOLAM

First light at the threshold. Pragya draws each answer as one unbroken line that can only loop around the dots of your own content.

 Concept C

PLACES, PLEASE

The site that isn't there yet. An empty black-box theatre where sections fly in from the rigging only when you ask for them.

 Concept G

THE LIQUID INTERFACE

It only reflects what's real. One drop of liquid metal becomes the website, and the metal can only shine with the site's own pages.

 Option 1 · plain-language

Same website. Different result.

The same website, twice, with the same visitor. One loses her. The one with FluidBuddha answers, shows and books a site survey.

 Option 2 · plain-language

While you were asleep

One night on your website, from 10:30 PM to 8:00 AM. You wake up to a hot lead, a quote already sent, and a summary of the night.

 Option 3 · plain-language

See it on your own website

Enter your website (or pick an example) and watch it get read, learned and brought to life. Then ask it what your customers ask.

 Option 5 · plain-language

Don't read about it. Talk to it.

The hero is the demo. Ask our own website anything, and each answer lights up one of the six things FluidBuddha does.
In these mockups Pragya's replies are scripted, so they can run with no backend. On the real site the same beats come from the live FluidBuddha agent. Sound is off by default. Turn it on (top right) to hear the browser's voice stand in for Pragya.
PLACES, PLEASE needs WebGL and THE LIQUID INTERFACE needs WebGL2: open them in Chrome or Edge with hardware acceleration on.
