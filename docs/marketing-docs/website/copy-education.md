## 3. Sections after the film

### 3.1 Try it in Hindi
**Ask it a question. In Hindi, if you like.**
Starter questions: "MBA की फ़ीस कितनी है?" · "Which programme suits me?" · "Scholarship kaise milegi?" · "How do I apply?"
Note: This demo answers about FluidBuddha. On your site it answers about your programmes.

### 3.2 What a student can do in one visit
| Step | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask | By voice or by typing, in Hindi or English. No form first. |
| 2 | Compare | Fees, eligibility and programmes in one table. |
| 3 | Pay the application fee | Through a payment link on your own Razorpay account. |
| 4 | Share with family | A summary page they can forward. |

### 3.3 What parents see
**The page the family judges you by.**
A plain summary: what was asked, what the answer was, the fee table, where each answer came
from, and how to apply. It opens on any phone and works even with scripts switched off.

### 3.4 What your counsellor receives
**A lead with its story.**
Card labels: *Name and contact* · *Course and city* · *What they asked* · *Score, with reasons* · *Whether the family opened the summary*
Line: Hot leads can alert your team in Slack.

### 3.5 Getting fees right
**Fee answers come from your pages, not from guesswork.**
- Fee answers quote your website and show the source.
- You can fix an approved fee answer so the same words are used every time.
- For payments, the amount can only come from the fee list you enter.
These are controls you set. Review them before each admission season.

### 3.6 Student privacy
- Emails, phone numbers and card numbers are hidden before the AI sees them.
- The microphone stays off until the student agrees. "Type instead" always works.
- You choose how long conversations are kept.
- You can delete one student's data by their email address.
- Names and street addresses are not hidden automatically.
- The product does not ask for age. Please review your policy on minors' data with your own legal adviser.

### 3.7 An example return
One extra enrolment worth ₹2,00,000 in first-year fees would cover the Growth plan
(₹33,000 a month) for about six months. *This is an example, not a result.* Measure your own
with the built-in test on your "application complete" page.

### 3.8 What it does not do
- English and Hindi only. Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and other languages are not supported yet.
- It works on your website. It does not run on WhatsApp.
- It does not replace counsellors. It gives them students who are ready.

### 3.9 Close

## 4. FAQ

| Role first | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Admissions | Can it answer in Hindi if our site is in English? | Yes. It understands Hindi and Hinglish, finds the answer in your English pages and replies in Hindi. |
| Admissions | What if a student asks something that is not on our site? | It says it does not know and offers to connect them with your team. You see these questions in a weekly list. |
| Leadership | Is student data safe? | Contact details are hidden before the AI sees them, the microphone needs consent, and you control retention and deletion. Names and addresses are not hidden automatically. |
| Marketing | Does it work on WordPress? | Yes, with a plugin. |
| Marketing | Can we test it before admission season? | Yes. Start on the Free plan and switch on the built-in test. |
| Counselling | Will I be compared with the AI? | No. It handles first questions at any hour so that your calls start with students who are ready. |
