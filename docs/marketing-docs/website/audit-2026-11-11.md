# Website audit and redesign (11 November 2026)

> Scope: every page of `fludi-buddha-website/web`: the four worlds (`/`, `/for/saas`,
> `/for/education`, `/for/enterprise`) and the older pages under `(site)` (pricing, demo, product,
> solutions, industries, developers, trust, legal, the Hindi pages).
> Method: the code was read in full; every page was rendered in headless Chrome at 1440 x 900 and
> 390 x 844, at several points of each film, with the console and network logged. Checked against
> the two design skills you named: **taste-skill** (`leonxlnx/taste-skill`, v2.5) and
> **impeccable** (`pbakaus/impeccable`, v4.8), and against the claims rules in
> [UseCases.md §10](../UseCases.md#10-claims-guidance-for-marketing).
> The skills' own scripts (impeccable's launcher, taste-skill's gate) were not run: they download
> or execute code. Their rules were applied by hand and checked in the browser.

**In one line.** The films are excellent and the rest of the site is not: two design systems sit
side by side, every section after the films uses the same template, a third of the pages show
broken images, and phones cannot reach the navigation.

## 2. Structure

### 2.1 Two sites in one

The four worlds and the older pages use different chrome. A visitor who clicks "Pricing" or
"Product" in any world lands in the other site.

| Part | Worlds | Older pages |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Sticky bar, 68 px, four links that change per world, a "Showing the … version" chip, flat gold button | Fixed transparent bar, 78 px, two dropdowns, "हिन्दी", "Book a demo" and a metallic gradient button |
| Logo | White or black horizontal mark | Gold horizontal mark |
| Footer | One line: name and four links | Six columns, a large gold logo, tagline and language links |
| Buttons | Flat, 13 x 22 px padding, weight 600 | Metallic gradient with a moving shine, weight 500 |
| Headings | Space Grotesk 600 | Space Grotesk 300 |
| Ask bar | None | Fixed at the bottom of every page |

### 2.4 Phones

World pages set `.nav ul` and the version chip to `display: none` under 900 px and offer no
menu. A phone visitor sees a logo, a Hindi button and one call to action, and cannot reach
Pricing, Product, Developers or another version. The plan's own rule ("always show which version
is on screen and a one-click way back") fails on phones.

## 3. Design

### 3.1 What works

- **The films.** The amber glass film (default) and the photographed night-to-morning film
  (Education) are the best things on the site. They are premium and specific to this product.
- **The morph.** Dissolving the page into gold dots and re-forming it as another world is a real
  "this site is fluid" moment.
- **The idea of the code-drawn films** (characters for SaaS, line drawings for Enterprise), and the
  care in the copy rules (every example figure is labelled; limits are stated).

### 3.2 What does not

| Finding | Rule it breaks | Seen on |
|---|---|---|
| After the film, every section is the same: small label, headline with a gold second half, a short grey paragraph, then a row of equal cards or a hairline list | taste 4.7 "Section-layout repetition", impeccable "identical card grids" | All four worlds (10 to 12 sections each) |
| A small uppercase label over every heading (about 75 across the worlds) and section numbers on Enterprise | impeccable craft floor: "A kicker or eyebrow above a heading … is a ban"; taste: max one per three sections | All pages |
| Gold gradient text in every headline | impeccable absolute ban "gradient text" | Default, SaaS, all older pages |
| Body text 14 px grey on black or cream; captions 11 to 12 px | impeccable: hierarchy through size and weight | All sections after the films |
| Large empty bands: sections half a screen of content in a full screen of space | taste "one section per screen, nothing sparse" | Default (talk, ourselves, limits), Enterprise (all) |
| Hero carries six or seven text elements (label, headline, sub, buttons, selector, note, role chips) | taste 4.7 "hero stack: max 4" | Default, Education |
| Default hero headline on three lines; sub-line 27 words | taste: headline max 2 lines, sub max 20 words | Default |
| Default and SaaS look like one site: same warm black, same gold, same section templates | Plan risk 5: "four worlds look like one site with different words" | `/` and `/for/saas` |
| Education and Enterprise both read as a cream or white document with the same templates | Same | `/for/education` and `/for/enterprise` |
| The SaaS character film is dim (lit characters on a grid of grey dots) and the Enterprise line drawings are thin and small; both read as unfinished rather than precise | impeccable: "with every check green, spend the page on the committed world" | `/for/saas`, `/for/enterprise` |
| Decorative grid paper behind the whole SaaS page | impeccable: "two-axis grid overlays need an actual canvas, map, blueprint" | `/for/saas` |
| Chapter index along the right edge set in 10 px mono capitals over the picture | Legibility | All films |
| A "1/08 · It talks" counter above each chapter headline | impeccable: numbered markers as scaffolding | All films |
| No moment after the hero: nothing to try, touch or watch respond | taste rule 5: "every page carries one dealt moment" | All four worlds |
| Browser surfaces left default (scrollbars, caret, underline offset) | impeccable craft floor "browser surfaces" | All pages |
| Every block on the older pages fades in on scroll | taste: "a card, row or tile that fades in on scroll" is never | All older pages |

## 5. The redesign

### 5.1 One site

- **One header and one footer** on every page, rendered once in the root layout so they persist
  across navigation and the morph. The header carries the logo, a **version switch** beside it
  (General, SaaS, Education, Banks and insurers), the same five links everywhere (Product,
  Solutions, Pricing, Developers, Trust), the Hindi switch where Hindi exists, and the world's own
  main button. Phones get a full-screen menu that includes the version switch.
- **The whole site follows the visitor's version.** The world is set on `<html>` before first
  paint (path for world pages, the remembered choice elsewhere), so Pricing seen from the SaaS
  version is in the SaaS look. This is the product's own persona re-theming, applied to its site.
- **One token system** in `globals.css`, with a block per world. The older pages are rebuilt on it
  and their broken posters replaced with frames from the two films.
- **Routes:** `/industries/saas`, `/industries/education` and `/enterprise` redirect to their
  worlds; `/hi` to `/?lang=hi`; `/hi/pricing` to `/pricing?lang=hi`; `/hi/demo` to `/demo`;
  `/customers` to `/about`. A branded 404. The sitemap lists every public page.
- **Ask Pragya**: the ask bar becomes a small light in the corner of every page that opens the
  conversation, like the product's own Companion design level.

### 5.2 Four worlds, four materials

| | Default | SaaS | Education | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Amber glass | Night console | Lamp to morning | The specification |
| Ground | Warm black, liquid-glass plates | Cool graphite, hairline cells | Lamp-lit dark to morning paper | White paper, ink, drafting grid behind drawings only |
| Type | Space Grotesk light, large | Space Grotesk medium, JetBrains Mono for data | Space Grotesk regular, larger body, Devanagari paired | Space Grotesk medium, smaller, spec numbers in mono |
| Corners | 18 px | 6 px | 26 px | 2 px |
| Gold | Hero words, main button, light | Data, live values, cursor light | Lamp glow, main button | Approval marks only |
| Motion | Slow, glowing | Quick, precise | Slow, warm | Moves once, then still |
| Signature moment | **The page answers back** | **Your lead, building live** | **Your English page, answered in Hindi** | **The rule console** |

### 5.3 The four signature moments

1. **Default: the page answers back.** A conversation stage right after the film. Pick a question
   and the page answers with its source; a price question draws a live calculator; the source
   chip takes you to the cited section and rings it in gold; "Show me the version for banks" turns
   the site into the Enterprise world. Four product pillars in one interaction. Labelled as a
   scripted preview until the live agent is connected.
2. **SaaS: your lead, building live.** A buyer's three questions as buttons. Each one answered in
   a chat column builds a lead card beside it: score with reasons, what was asked, pages seen. A
   switch shows the same lead as the Slack message and as the signed-webhook JSON. The price
   question brings a seat slider. Nothing leaves the browser.
3. **Education: your English page, answered in Hindi.** An English fee page on one side, a phone on
   the other. Ask "MBA की फ़ीस कितनी है?" and the answer types out in Hindi while a gold line runs
   from it to the sentence on the English page it came from. Hinglish and English questions too.
4. **Enterprise: the rule console.** Five controls you can switch (approved components only,
   prices from your systems, excluded pages, pinned notice, review everything). Run the check on a
   generated page and watch each block pass, or be left out with its reason, then the audit-log
   lines appear. The claim "you write the rules, the product enforces them and shows you what it
   did", shown rather than said.

Each world also keeps its film, tightened: no counter above each chapter, a readable chapter rail,
stronger contrast in the SaaS and Enterprise drawings, and a pointer light over the SaaS hero.
