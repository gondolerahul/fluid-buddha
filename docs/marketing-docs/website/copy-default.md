## 3. Sections after the film

### 3.1 Talk to it
**Don't read about it. Talk to it.**
This site runs on FluidBuddha. Ask it anything, out loud or by typing.
Starter questions: "How much does it cost?" · "Show me how it works" · "क्या ये हिंदी में बात करता है?" · "Book a demo"

### 3.2 What it does
| Card | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | It talks | Visitors ask by voice or text. It starts replying straight away, in English or Hindi. |
| 2 | It shows | It draws diagrams, charts, comparison tables and calculators as it explains. |
| 3 | It takes them there | It opens the right page, scrolls to the right section and highlights it. |
| 4 | It uses only your content | Every answer comes from your website, with a link to the source. |
| 5 | It books and sells | Meetings, quotes, trials and payment links, each confirmed by the visitor first. |
| 6 | It proves its value | A built-in test compares visitors who had it with visitors who did not. |

### 3.3 It fits each visitor
**Your pages change for each visitor.**
A visitor who came from a pricing ad sees pricing first. A visitor who came for security sees
security first. FluidBuddha builds these versions from the components you have approved. It
never writes its own HTML, and prices come from your systems, not from the AI.
Three labels under the picture: *Came for pricing* · *Came for security* · *Came back a second time*
Small print: Your own page shows until the fitted version is ready. Search engines always see your own page.

### 3.4 Who it is for
**Pick the one closest to you.**
| Door | Line |
|---|---|
| B2B SaaS | Buyers on your pricing page at 11 pm, answered. |
| Education | Students answered at night, in Hindi or English. |
| Banks, insurers and fintechs | AI on your website, inside your rules. |
Also: Real estate · Healthcare · Performance marketing · Agencies · Catalogues

### 3.5 How to start
**Three steps. It can be answering the same day.**
1. Add one line of code to your website.
2. FluidBuddha reads your site.
3. It starts answering.

### 3.6 Pricing
**Pay for conversations, not seats.**
Free: 100 conversations a month · Growth: ₹33,000 a month for 2,000 · Scale: ₹1,25,000 a month for 10,000 · Enterprise: by arrangement
Link: See full pricing

### 3.7 We use it ourselves
**Before launch:** Launching on our own sites first.
We are putting FluidBuddha on our own websites before we ask you to put it on yours. This
page will list each one as it goes live.
**After launch (per site, only when live):** Running on buddhalab.in · hirebuddha.in · chantingbuddha.in. Open any of them and talk to it.

### 3.8 What it does not do
- It speaks English and Hindi only.
- It works on websites. It is not a WhatsApp, phone or app agent.
- It answers from your content with sources. That is a strong guardrail, not a guarantee.

## 4. FAQ

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Will it make things up? | It answers from your website and shows its sources. When it cannot find an answer, it says so and offers to connect the visitor with your team. |
| How long does setup take? | You add one line of code and it reads your site. It can be answering the same day. |
| Does it slow my site down? | The main script is about 38.6 kB. Other parts load only when they are used. |
| Which languages? | English and Hindi. |
| What does it cost? | Free covers 100 conversations a month. Growth is ₹33,000 a month for 2,000 conversations. |
| Can I see whether it works? | Yes. It can hold back one visitor in ten and compare the two groups on your own goal. |
