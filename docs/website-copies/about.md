## Body Copy (PAS Framework)

### **The Problem: The 1995 Document Index**
Look at the modern web. We have autonomous cars, LLMs that code entire programs, and real-time multiplayer applications. Yet, when you visit a marketing website, you are looking at the exact same format designed in 1995: a static, silent index of documents. The website sits there, cold and passive, expecting the visitor to do all the cognitive heavy lifting—clicking menus, translating jargon, and scrolling hoping to find answers. 

### **The Agitation: The Disconnect in Digital Experience**
This passive layout is a massive disconnect. Companies spend millions building polished software products, only to sell them through rigid, unresponsive brochureware. Standard chatbots try to bridge this gap, but they fail because they are separated from the page. They sit in a tiny chat container in the corner, unable to see what's on the screen, unable to scroll, and unable to compile visuals. They are text-based tape-players, not active representatives.

### **The Solution: Giving AI its Eyes, Ears, and Hands**
We built **flUIdBuddha** to bring the concept of **Presence** back to the web. 

We believe software should feel like a calm, high-fidelity conversation. Our framework replaces rigid structures with **Pragya**—a living, voice-first agentic presence that behaves like a consultative sales partner:
*   **The Eyes:** Pragya understands exactly what the user is looking at and tracks viewport state.
*   **The Ears:** Pragya captures natural voice amplitude and supports hands-free interruption (Barge-in) using local VAD thresholding.
*   **The Hands:** Pragya scrolls, highlights, and compiles custom visual interfaces (ROI tools, custom checklists, comparison grids) on the fly to support her narration.

Our team is composed of seasoned designers, cognitive psychologists, and backend engineers dedicated to building a web that adapts to humans, not the other way around.

## Our Core Beliefs
1.  **Clarity Over Clutter:** We reject complex buttons, chrome, and status bars. Pragya communicates her states—dormant, listening, thinking, speaking—solely through the breathing rhythms of her Gold and Silver Aura.
2.  **Zero Hallucinations, Guaranteed:** We refuse to build "clever" AI that lies. Pragya is strictly grounded in pgvector databases via the CORTEX engine. If she doesn't know, she'll capture an inquiry and step aside.
3.  **Visual Respect:** We apply a translucent blur crossfade veil to mask layout updates, ensuring the website transitions smoothly in 300ms without visual flashing.
