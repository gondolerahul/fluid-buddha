## Body Copy (PAS Framework)

### **The Problem: The AI Budget Drain**
Hiring a 24/7 human sales and support team to qualify leads globally is incredibly expensive. However, building your own custom voice-AI solution costs hundreds of thousands in engineering hours. Even if you build a basic integration, standard LLM API fees quickly balloon. Every time a user asks: *"How much does this cost?"*, your server sends a full query to a heavy model, costing you cents per turn. At scale, this makes voice-AI financially unsustainable.

### **The Agitation: Wasted Repetitive API Calls**
Over 70% of website inquiries are repetitive questions about pricing, features, integrations, and setup. Sending these same questions to heavy, expensive reasoning models over and over again is like paying a consultant to read your FAQ page out loud. You are bleeding budget on routine traffic, while still losing conversions because of latency.

### **The Solution: Cost-Aware Conversational Intelligence**
flUIdBuddha’s pricing plans are designed to pay for themselves. Our architecture includes:
*   **Episodic Replay Cache:** If a user query matches a previously answered question (cosine similarity >= 0.92), the system bypasses the cognitive loop entirely and replays the cached audio and layout instantly. Cost to you: **$0.00**.
*   **Dual-Model Routing:** Simple inputs route to Gemini 1.5 Flash. Heavy calculations route to Gemini 1.5 Pro. This reduces standard LLM costs by up to **80%**.
