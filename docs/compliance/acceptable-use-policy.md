# Acceptable use policy (staff and contractors)

**Owner:** Security lead · **Review:** yearly · **Version:** 1.0 (5 Nov 2026, draft)

Everyone with access to FluidBuddha systems or data agrees to this when they
join and yearly after.

## Do

- Use company accounts with **two-factor** for every work system; keep
  passwords in the company password manager.
- Keep your device encrypted, locked when unattended (5 minutes), updated, and
  running supported software.
- Report lost devices, phishing, suspected incidents and policy breaches to the
  Security lead **immediately**.
- Use test data and test keys in development; anonymise any production data you
  must use for debugging, and delete it afterwards.
- Lock your screen in shared spaces; be mindful of what's visible on calls.

## Don't

- Share accounts, passwords, tokens or keys — with anyone, including colleagues.
- Put secrets or customer data in the repository, tickets, chat, AI tools or
  personal storage. (CI scans every commit for secrets.)
- Open customer data unless supporting that customer at their request or
  handling an incident; never browse it out of curiosity.
- Copy production data to a personal device.
- Install unvetted software or browser extensions on devices used for work.
- Turn off security controls (disk encryption, updates, antivirus, two-factor).

## AI tools

Code assistants and other AI tools may be used on our own code. Customer data,
production logs with customer content, and secrets must never be pasted into
them.

## Leaving

Return company devices and remove company data from personal ones by your last
day; your access ends then (see [access control](access-control-policy.md)).

Breaches may lead to losing access and disciplinary action.
