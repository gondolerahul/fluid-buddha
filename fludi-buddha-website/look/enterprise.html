02Controls

You write the rules. The product enforces them and shows you what it did.

  1. 2.1
    Approved componentsThe AI may use only components an admin has approved.
  2. 2.2
    Field rulesEach field is marked: the AI may write it, or it must come from your data.
  3. 2.3
    Site rulesRules you write override everything else the agent has been told.
  4. 2.4
    Fixed and blocked answersAn approved answer is repeated word for word. A blocked question is never answered.
  5. 2.5
    Excluded pagesCheckout, cart, legal, terms and privacy are excluded by default.
  6. 2.6
    Required noticesContent you pin appears on every generated page, unchanged.
  7. 2.7
    Review queueA share of generated pages goes to a reviewer. For a pilot it can be every page.
  8. 2.8
    Audit logEvery console change and sign-in, kept for one year, searchable, exportable.
